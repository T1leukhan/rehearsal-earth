Rehearsal Earth

An engineering analog atlas for rehearsing the Moon and Mars on Earth

Where on Earth can we rehearse the Moon and Mars, and how far can we trust it?

Before a rover or a lander flies, engineers test it somewhere on Earth that is as close as possible to where it is going. No place on Earth is a perfect match. This atlas uses NASA data to measure how close each site really is, and shows where the analogy breaks.

Measured numbers only.
